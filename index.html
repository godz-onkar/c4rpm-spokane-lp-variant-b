Common questions

What does it cost?

It depends on the joint and the treatment you need. Dr. Lewis gives you a price for your plan at your consultation, before anything is done.

Does insurance or Medicare cover this?

Usually not. Insurance and Medicare tend to cover surgery but not regenerative treatment, so plan on paying for it yourself. You can typically use a health savings account (HSA) here, and payment plans through Cherry include 0% APR options if you qualify.

Am I too old for this?

Age alone doesn’t rule you out. Dr. Lewis looks at the joint and your overall health.

Is this stem cell therapy?

It can be. Bone marrow concentrate, drawn from the back of your hip, contains your own stem cells. Some people need it, and others only need a platelet treatment. Dr. Lewis tells you which at your consultation.

Is this surgery? How long is the recovery?

No. It’s done in our clinic, and there’s no surgical recovery. Expect some stiffness and soreness for three to four days. Most people start to notice improvement around six to eight weeks, with gains continuing over several months. Individual results vary.

How many visits will I need?

You’ll come in for a consultation, then a treatment day, with follow-up by phone, in the office or by secure message. If you have a bone marrow treatment, you’ll need someone to drive you home that day.

What if I’m not a candidate?

Dr. Lewis only recommends treatment when he believes it can make a real difference for you. If it can’t, he’ll tell you why.

Can I fill this out for a parent or spouse?

Yes. Answer the questions for them, and you’re welcome to join the call.

Are you ready to get back to what you love?

Answer a few short questions. If it looks like a fit, you’ll book a free call with our team.
