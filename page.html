How it works

  1. 1

    Answer a few questions.

    Tell us where it hurts, how long it’s been going on and how it’s affecting your day.

  2. 2

    A free call with our team.

    You pick a time, and a patient care coordinator calls you then to talk through what you’ve tried and what you want to get back to doing. Calls take about 30 minutes, and nothing is treated or decided on the call.

  3. 3

    A visit with one of our physicians.

    If the call shows it’s worth a closer look, you’ll come in to see one of our board-certified physicians. They review your history, examine you and look at any imaging and blood work, since your overall health affects healing. Then they tell you whether you’re a candidate and which treatment fits.

Common questions

What does it cost?

It depends on the joint and the treatment you need, so there’s no one price. The physician gives you the price for your plan at your consultation, before anything is done.

Does insurance or Medicare cover this?

Usually not. Insurance and Medicare tend to cover surgery but not regenerative treatment, so plan on paying for it yourself. You can typically use a health savings account (HSA) here, and we offer monthly payment plans through Cherry, including 0% APR options if you qualify.

Am I too old for this?

No. Age alone doesn’t rule you out, and many of our patients are in their 60s, 70s and 80s. The physician looks at the joint and your overall health.

Is this stem cell therapy?

Sometimes. One treatment uses bone marrow concentrate, drawn from the back of your hip, which contains your own stem cells. Another uses platelets from your own blood (PRP). The physician tells you which one fits you at your consultation.

Is this surgery? How long is the recovery?

No. Treatment is an injection, done in our clinic, so there’s no surgical recovery. Expect some stiffness and soreness for three to four days. Most people start to notice improvement around six to eight weeks, with gains continuing over several months. Individual results vary.

How many visits will I need?

You’ll come in for a consultation, then a treatment day, with follow-up by phone, in the office or by secure message. Some treatments are a series of injections, and the physician tells you if yours is. After a bone marrow treatment, you’ll need someone to drive you home.

What if I’m not a candidate?

The physician will tell you, and explain why. We only recommend treatment when we believe it can make a real difference for you.

Can I fill this out for a parent or spouse?

Yes. Answer the questions for them, and you’re welcome to join the call.

Do I need a referral?

You don’t need a referral from another doctor. Answer the questions on this page or call (509) 588-7340 to get started.

Ready to take the first step?

Answer a few short questions. If our approach looks like a fit, you’ll pick a time for a free call with our team.
